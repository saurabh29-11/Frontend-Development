About Jaipur

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is known as the "Pink City" for its distinctly colored buildings. Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, it’s a blend of royal heritage, culture, and modernity.

Culture and Heritage

Jaipur’s culture is a colorful mix of art, traditions, and festivals. The people of Jaipur celebrate life with dance, music, and grand fairs like Teej and Gangaur. Handicrafts and textiles here reflect centuries-old craftsmanship.

Famous Tourist Spots

Some of Jaipur’s must-visit attractions include the Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, and the City Palace. Each monument tells a story of architectural brilliance and royal elegance.

Delicious Rajasthani Food

From Dal Baati Churma to Ghewar, Jaipur offers authentic Rajasthani dishes that capture the true essence of the region’s flavors and hospitality.
